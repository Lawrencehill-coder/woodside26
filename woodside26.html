Section 02
Blocking Issue — Drainage Easement
CRITICAL BLOCK: Contiguous Property Drainage Easement The engineer (KJE / Kevin Ware) has confirmed that a contiguous property requires drainage access across this site. The drainage easement must be legally established and recorded before the project can proceed to platting, engineering, or rezoning. This is not a minor condition — it is a project-stopping impediment until resolved.
INVESTOR DISPUTE — 4 YEARS OUTSTANDING Clif Tinder has disclosed that the drainage easement issue is tied to a dispute between two individual investors that has been ongoing for approximately four years. The dispute directly affects the ability to grant or record the necessary easement. Timeline to resolution is unknown.
PEDISON POSITION Lawrence Hill is monitoring only. No capital commitment, no advisory engagement, and no formal due diligence will be initiated until the drainage easement dispute is resolved and the easement is recorded. Follow up with engineer Kevin Ware after resolution. Do not advance this deal to internal committee while the block is active.
